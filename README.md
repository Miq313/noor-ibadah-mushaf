# Noor ʿIbādah muṣḥaf

The muṣḥaf that the Noor ʿIbādah app downloads the first time its Qurʾān reader opens, so it doesn't have to be
inside the app itself.

- **`mushaf.pdf`** (release [`mushaf-1`](../../releases/tag/mushaf-1)): al-Muṣḥaf al-Sharīf, the Madinah print of
  1441 AH, riwāyat Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim, from the King Fahd Glorious Qurʾān Printing Complex. 604 pages.
- For phone screens each page is cropped to its text frame and centred on an even margin, and the ḥizb medallions
  are kept, smaller, beside the frame. The text itself is as printed.
- 65,551,916 bytes, SHA-256 `b75e8b75ed24bf3e1f938be4c9bf69df7fcdecee8a1dfb47b9b0e44e9a6d7876`.

The King Fahd Complex publishes the muṣḥaf free for personal, commercial and publishing use at
<https://dm.qurancomplex.gov.sa/>.
